## Kelvara DW-300 dishwasher (manual id: kelvara-dw300)
- Model sticker location (p. 4): along the right edge of the inner door, visible when the door is open.
- Model code format: "DW-300" followed by a 4-character variant, e.g. DW-300-K7S2.

### Quick checks (p. 16), always before tools
1. Door fully latched (press until it clicks).
2. Delay Start is off (no clock icon on the display).
3. Child Lock is off (hold "Lock" for 3 seconds; panel label "Lock").
4. Power and water supply on.

### Error codes (p. 17-19)
| Code | Meaning | Page | Simplest-first causes | DIY? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E24 | Water not draining | 18 | 1. Clogged filter (most common) 2. Kinked drain hose 3. Disposal knockout plug not removed (new installs) | Yes (filter, hose) |
| E15 | Water detected in base (leak) | 19 | Leak inside the base pan | **Technician only** (do not tilt or open the base) |
| E09 | Heater fault | 19 | Internal heating element | **Technician only** (internal electrical) |

### Clean the filter (fixes E24), p. 18. Time: about 15 minutes. Tools: towel, soft brush, small bowl.
1. Turn off the dishwasher and unplug it. **GATE: confirm unplugged.**
2. Lay a towel in front of the door to catch water.
3. Pull out the bottom rack.
4. Twist the round filter left (counterclockwise) and lift it out.
5. Rinse the filter under warm water and scrub gently with a soft brush.
6. Check the sump opening for debris and remove it by hand. **GATE: confirm power is still off.**
7. Put the filter back and twist right until it locks.
8. Slide the rack back, plug in, and run the "Rinse" cycle (panel label "Rinse"). Verify: water drains with no E24.

### Check the drain hose (second cause for E24), p. 18
1. Unplug. **GATE: confirm unplugged.**
2. Open the cabinet under the sink.
3. Find the grey drain hose and straighten any kinks or bends.
4. Plug in and run "Rinse". Verify: no E24.

## Kelvara WM-500 front-load washer (manual id: kelvara-wm500)
- Model sticker location (p. 3): inside the door rim, at the top.

### Quick checks (p. 20)
1. Door fully closed (panel shows the lock icon when a cycle starts).
2. Drain hose not kinked behind the washer.
3. Load not too heavy or unbalanced.

### Error codes (p. 21-25)
| Code | Meaning | Page | Simplest-first causes | DIY? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E21 | Slow drain | 24 | 1. Clogged pump filter (coin trap) 2. Kinked hose | Yes |
| E31 | Drain pump fault | 25 | Pump motor failed (if E31 remains after cleaning the pump filter) | Filter clean: Yes. Pump replacement: **Technician only** |
| dE | Door lock fault | 22 | Door latch or lock assembly | Close door firmly: Yes. Lock assembly: **Technician only** |

### Clean the pump filter (E21, first step for E31), p. 24. Time: about 20 minutes. Tools: shallow tray, towels.
1. Turn off the washer and unplug it. **GATE: confirm unplugged.**
2. Wait 10 minutes if a hot wash just ran (water may be hot). **GATE: confirm water is cool.**
3. Open the small panel at the bottom front right (panel label "Filter").
4. Place the tray and towels under it. Pull out the small drain tube and let water drain into the tray.
5. Twist the pump filter left and pull it out slowly.
6. Remove lint, coins, or hair. Rinse the filter.
7. Twist the filter back in until tight, close the tube and panel.
8. Plug in and run "Drain & Spin" (panel label "Drain & Spin"). Verify: no error.
If E31 appears again after this: pump replacement, **technician only** (p. 25).
